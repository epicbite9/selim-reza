AI & LANGUAGE

Why Bengali Context Matters in AI Evaluation

Bengali AI evaluation requires more than checking spelling. Meaning can change through tone, cultural references, honorifics, idioms, and sentence structure. A good evaluator checks whether an answer sounds natural to Bengali speakers and whether it respects the original context.

QUALITY REVIEW

Five Things to Check in an AI-Generated Answer

A useful review should examine factual accuracy, relevance, completeness, clarity, and instruction-following. An answer can be grammatically correct but still fail because it ignores the user’s request or confidently presents unsupported information.
